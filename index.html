Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between interpreting and translation?

Interpreting is spoken: an interpreter helps people communicate live, in person, by phone, or by video. Translation is written: a translator converts documents such as forms, letters, and notices from one language to another. Apple Tree Interpreters offers both.

Which languages do you cover?

We serve Oromo, Amharic, Somali, Spanish, Hmong, Karen, Vietnamese, Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, and many more. If you don't see your language, contact us and we'll let you know what we can arrange.

Where do you provide services?

We provide in-person interpreting throughout the Twin Cities area, including Minneapolis and St. Paul. Phone, video, and translation services can be provided remotely.

How quickly can I get an interpreter?

Most requests are confirmed within 2 hours, and we're available 24/7, including weekends, for urgent and emergency needs.
