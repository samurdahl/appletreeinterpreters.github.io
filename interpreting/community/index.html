Frequently asked questions

Do you work with government agencies and programs?

We welcome work with government agencies and programs. Contact us to discuss your requirements, which may include vendor registration, contracts, background checks, or billing procedures, and we will let you know what we can accommodate.

Can you interpret at public meetings and community events?

Yes. We can provide in-person interpreting for meetings, workshops, and events, and phone or video interpreting when it fits. Tell us the date, location, and languages needed.

Do you translate outreach materials?

Yes. We translate flyers, notices, forms, and other materials. Request a quote on our translation page.

How do I set up recurring services for a program?

Contact us with your program's languages, schedule, and requirements. We will follow up to discuss a plan that fits.
