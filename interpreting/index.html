Frequently asked questions

What information do I need to request an interpreter?

The language, the date and time, the location (or video link), the type of setting, and a contact person. Our online form walks you through each item.

How quickly can I get an interpreter?

Most requests are confirmed within 2 hours. We are available 24/7, including weekends, for urgent and emergency needs. For the fastest response, call us.

Which languages do you cover?

We serve Oromo, Amharic, Somali, Spanish, Hmong, Karen, Vietnamese, Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, and many more. If you do not see your language, contact us and we will let you know what we can arrange.

Where do you provide in-person interpreting?

Throughout the Twin Cities area, including Minneapolis and St. Paul. Phone and video interpreting can be provided from anywhere.
