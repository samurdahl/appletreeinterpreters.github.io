Frequently asked questions

Can a family member interpret instead?

Qualified interpreters are generally recommended over family members or friends, who may leave things out, add opinions, or find it hard to relay sensitive information. A professional interpreter also protects privacy.

How do I request a medical interpreter?

Use our secure online form for requests with patient details, or call us for urgent needs. Please do not include patient names or medical details in email or text.

Do you provide interpreters for emergencies?

We are available 24/7, including weekends, and can often respond quickly to urgent needs. Call (612) 400-8903 for the fastest response.

Are healthcare organizations required to provide interpreters?

Many healthcare organizations are required by federal and state rules to provide language assistance. Check with your compliance team about your specific obligations. We can help you meet them.
