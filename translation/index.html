The languages, the type of document, roughly how long it is, and your deadline. We will confirm details and a secure way to share the document after you submit the form.

What is the difference between interpreting and translation?

Interpreting is spoken and happens live, in person, by phone, or by video. Translation is written. Apple Tree Interpreters offers both.
